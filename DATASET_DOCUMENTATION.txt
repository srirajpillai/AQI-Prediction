================================================================================
AIRFLOW AI — AQI PREDICTION MODEL TRAINING DATASETS DOCUMENTATION
================================================================================

This document outlines the datasets utilized (both historically and newly added) 
for training the AirFlow AI predictive models, including the ML inference engine, 
sub-index weighting, and the cross-city transfer learning spatial kernel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: DATASETS USED SO FAR (BASELINE & API INTEGRATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open-Meteo Historical Air Quality & Weather Archive
   - Description: A comprehensive historical dataset of 10km-resolution air quality and meteorological variables across the globe.
   - Reason Used: Essential for establishing the baseline correlations between atmospheric conditions (temperature, wind, humidity, pressure) and real-time pollutant concentration (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2).
   - Parameter(s): Temperature, Humidity, Wind Speed, Wind Direction, PM2.5, PM10
   - Link: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api

2. WAQI (World Air Quality Index) Real-Time & Historical Data
   - Description: Ground-level station data from over 130 countries.
   - Reason Used: Used to calibrate our "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" thresholds and validate the ML model's dominant pollutant logic against actual government EPA sensors.
   - Parameter(s): US-EPA AQI standard, Dominant Pollutants
   - Link: https://aqicn.org/data-platform/register/

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: NEW KAGGLE DATASETS ADDED FOR ENHANCED TRAINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To further optimize our model's accuracy, reduce overhead, and accurately predict 
complex real-world factors (like thermal inversions and geopolitical events), the 
following datasets have been added to our training pipeline:

3. Air Quality Data in India (2015 - 2020) (Kaggle)
   - Description: Hourly and daily city-level air quality data for major Indian cities.
   - Reason Used: Crucial for training the model on extreme pollution events, particularly the winter smog and crop-burning (agricultural) impacts in North India (Delhi, Gurugram). It improves the "Meteorological" and "Agricultural" event detection in our worker node.
   - Parameter(s): PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NOx, CO, Benzene, Toluene
   - Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india

4. Global Air Pollution Dataset (Kaggle)
   - Description: Comprehensive dataset mapping AQI values globally alongside lat/lon coordinates.
   - Reason Used: Used specifically to train the "Cross-City Transfer Learning" weights. It helps the model understand spatial decay—how much pollution from City A transfers to City B based on distance.
   - Parameter(s): AQI, Latitude, Longitude, Country
   - Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hasibalmuzdadid/global-air-pollution-dataset

5. Delhi Weather and Air Quality (Continuous) (Kaggle)
   - Description: A highly granular dataset combining continuous weather metrics with air pollution levels in New Delhi.
   - Reason Used: Used to train our "Atmospheric Insights" (Hourly AI Forecast) engine. It teaches the model how Nocturnal Thermal Inversions and Morning Boundary Layer Stagnation cause severe PM2.5 spikes.
   - Parameter(s): Wind Speed, Wind Direction, Temperature, PM2.5 (Hourly)
   - Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mahirkukreja/delhi-weather-data

6. Wildfire & Air Quality Index Dataset (Kaggle)
   - Description: Datasets correlating historical wildfire locations/intensities with downwind AQI degradation.
   - Reason Used: Integrated to train the `DETECT_FACTORS` ML logic for "Natural Events". It allows the application to dynamically predict severe AQI multipliers when the model detects extreme dry winds coupled with sudden PM10/PM2.5 spikes (hallmarks of smoke advection).
   - Parameter(s): Fire Radiative Power (FRP), Aerosol Optical Depth, PM2.5, AQI
   - Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tunguz/us-wildfires

================================================================================
HOW TO USE FOR OFFLINE TRAINING:
If retraining the JavaScript worker.js ML weights offline via Python:
1. Download the CSVs from the Kaggle links above.
2. Run a regression/random forest model against the target variables (AQI).
3. Export the resulting feature importances (e.g., Wind=0.65, Temp=0.35) into 
   the `worker.js` script to update the real-time inference multipliers.
