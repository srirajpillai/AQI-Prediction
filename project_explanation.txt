             AIRFLOW AI — THE SIMPLEST EXPLANATION OF THIS WEBSITE
========================================================================

What is this website?
---------------------
Think of this website like a weather app, but instead of telling you if it's 
going to rain, it tells you how clean or dirty the air is that you are breathing 
right now, and what it will look like tomorrow. 

We call this the Air Quality Index (AQI). 
- A low number (like 0 to 50) means the air is clean and safe (Green).
- A high number (like 150 or more) means the air is dirty and bad for you (Red).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
How does the website work in 5 simple steps?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IT GRABS REAL-TIME DATA (Checking the Air)
   When you search for a city, the website immediately asks official weather 
   stations and satellites: "Hey, what is the temperature, how fast is the 
   wind blowing, and how many tiny dirt particles are in the air right now?"

2. IT CHECKS THE NEIGHBORS (The Smoke-Blowing Analogy)
   Imagine you are sitting in your yard, and your neighbor starts a campfire. 
   If the wind is blowing from their yard to yours, you will smell smoke. 
   Our website does the exact same thing:
   - It looks at 5 cities nearby.
   - It checks their air quality.
   - It looks at the wind direction to see if the dirty air from those cities 
     is being blown toward your city!

3. IT KNOWS THE DAILY PATTERNS (Daily Habits)
   Air pollution has a daily routine, just like us:
   - In the morning (rush hour traffic), the air gets dirtier.
   - In the afternoon (sunny and warm), the air gets cleaner because hot air 
     rises and carries the dust away.
   - In the evening, the air gets dirty again.
   The website uses this pattern to guess how the air will change hour-by-hour.

4. IT LOOKS FOR WEATHER TRAPS AND CLEANERS (Weather Helpers)
   The website looks at the weather forecast to adjust its predictions:
   - Rain: Acts like a giant shower that washes the dust out of the sky. (Cleaner)
   - Heavy Fog: Acts like a heavy blanket that traps smog on the ground. (Trap)
   - No Wind: Stagnant air means pollution just sits there and builds up. (Trap)

5. IT EXPLAINS "WHY" (The Receipt)
   Instead of just giving you a random air quality number, the website gives 
   you a list (called the SHAP Graph) that explains *why* the air is bad. 
   It's like a supermarket receipt:
   - Cars and Traffic: +25 points (Bad)
   - Factory Smoke: +15 points (Bad)
   - Strong Winds: -20 points (Good, cleaned the air!)
   - Final Air Score: 20 points (Nice and clean!)

Why is this version (Version 1) special?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
It is 100% self-contained. There is no complicated software to install, no 
servers to set up, and no programming knowledge needed. 

You just double-click the "index.html" file, and it opens instantly in Chrome, 
Safari, or Edge. It does all the math calculations inside a hidden background 
helper (called a Web Worker) so your browser never freezes or lags.
